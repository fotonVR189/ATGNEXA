The Lab

What Is a fotonVR Lab?

A fotonVR Lab is a VR-based learning setup schools use alongside regular teaching. Students wear a headset and interact with 3D simulations of topics from their syllabus.

Instead of relying only on printed materials and diagrams, students can explore concepts through immersive visual experiences.

FAQ

Common Questions About Immersive Learning

What is a VR lab for schools?

A learning setup where students use VR headsets to explore lessons through interactive 3D experiences alongside regular classroom teaching.

Do teachers need technical training?

Teacher onboarding can help educators become comfortable operating the learning setup and integrating it into classroom activities. Ask ATGNEXA about available training.

What subjects are covered?

Potential subject areas include Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Geography, History and other science topics. Available content depends on the platform's current library.

Is the content suitable for school-age students?

Schools should review the available content, age suitability, supervision requirements and usage guidance with the provider before classroom use.
